Fonts used by the site. The .woff2 files are NOT in the repository (their licences do not allow
redistributing them); put them in client/public/fonts (see the README, "Fonts"). Names to use:
shizuoka-cyberpunk.woff2, heyam.woff2, candice.woff2, sniglet.woff2.

Heyam -> use this font for the phrase "Manche suivante !" spoken by the presenter during the transition with the clouds. It must be displayed really big.
Shizuoka Cyberpunk -> title "Mini-jeux entre amis" and titles of the games
Use the default font for the rest.
Qui est le + ? (whos-most):
  Candice (Personal Use) -> the "Qui est le +" of the question and the texts placed on the scribbles (host's buttons, vote, hints). "Personal Use" licence: not redistributable, to check for a website.
  Sniglet -> the adjective of the question, the vote count and the small labels under the heads.
  Shizuoka Cyberpunk -> the nickname, on the top edge of each frame.
Shizuoka Cyberpunk -> also for the screen titles of the games (role of Among Legends, "Tu es le démineur", "BOUM" of Defusal).
